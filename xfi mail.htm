Changes are coming for your Comcast.net email account

 
We’re closing all  Comcast.net email accounts with insufficient user information in the last two years, and we noticed you have at least one incomplete billing information associated with your Xfinity account.

This email account will automatically close in about 90 days and any messages and data will be deleted unless you act soon.
 
 
 
Here are your options
 
•
If you want to keep your Xfinity account(s) and prevent it from closing, just sign in and update account information within the next 90 days.
•
If you'd like to download your email data before your email account closes, you can get started here: https://export.xfinity.com/
•
If you don’t want to keep access to your inactive Comcast.net email(s), there’s nothing you need to do.
 
We recommend you review your saved emails on file to make sure they are up to date and that you don’t have an inactive Comcast.net email listed as your preferred email.
